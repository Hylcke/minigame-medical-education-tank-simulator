The exam after the training

Five levels against the clock

Get a pen, paper and a calculator. Each level shows you a picture and a few facts. Work out the answer on paper, type it in and fire. The formulas stay on screen, so it is about doing the sums right and fast. Levels 1, 4 and 5 need you to read the picture.

Choose a code and start

    How you are scored

    Accuracy
    600 points. Full marks within 5% on range and 10% on hold over and lead. Then it drops fast, to nothing at 15% out on range, 35% on hold over and 30% on lead.
    Speed
    300 points. Full marks under the par time, nothing at four times par. Scaled down hard by your accuracy, so a fast guess scores nothing.
    Hit
    100 points if the round hits and your answer was at least half right.

    Each level is worth 1000, so the best possible score is 5000. The clock starts when you press Start on each level and stops when you fire.
