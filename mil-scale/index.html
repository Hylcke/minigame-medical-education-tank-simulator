Range by mil scale

Every vehicle has a known size. Measure how many mils it covers in the binoculars, then range = size × 1000 ÷ mils.

Drag the view or use the arrow keys to line the vehicle up with the scale. Small ticks are 1 mil. Numbered ticks are 10 mils. Measure the hull, not the gun.
